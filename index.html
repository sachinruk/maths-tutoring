About me

I'm Sachin. I've been tutoring Mathematics Advanced, Extension 1 and Extension 2 (the old 2, 3 and 4 unit courses) for more than 20 years.

I went to James Ruse Agricultural High School and finished with a UAI of 99.25 (the UAI was the ATAR's predecessor). I went on to complete a PhD in artificial intelligence and statistics, and I now work as a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Canva, where calculus, probability and statistics are part of my everyday work.

In my lessons, students learn why a method works, not just which steps to follow. Students who understand the ideas can handle the unfamiliar questions that separate a Band 5 from a Band 6 or an E3 from an E4.
